# kagiboy pitch video: script v2

For the Colosseum Crypto World's Fair submission (3:00 limit). Voice: hazy, first person, conversational. Same story and facts as the website, told personally; it deliberately doesn't repeat the site's slogans. Every claim stays honest: testnets only, swaps never sent, hardware in progress, audits before sale, no funding ask.

Video project: `video/` (Remotion). Record one audio file per section (`s1`–`s9`, e.g. `s1.m4a`); the scenes re-time to your recording.

### 1 · The memory
**Visual:** VHS memories: the tape deck, a car window, a kid at a window, then hazy's own Game Boy photo, cropped to the screen.

> My dad found it at a flea market. I was too young to know what a Nintendo was. I just knew it was mine. The game was Super Mario Land. I never beat it. I didn't care. I had the music, and a whole world I could carry around.

### 2 · Now
**Visual:** Cold present: the Shibuya crossing, then the night train with a phone that keeps buzzing and asks to approve.

> Now the most important things I own are numbers on a phone. A phone that buzzes all day with offers, links, and people who want something from me. And when it asks me to approve something, it's the same phone telling me what I'm approving. I just have to trust it. Every single time.

### 3 · The drawer
**Visual:** Inside a drawer looking up: the room's light narrows to a slit over a plain hardware wallet, then black.

> The safe answer is a hardware wallet. Most of them end up in a drawer.

### 4 · The idea
**Visual:** Hard cut to white: "The most honest screen I've ever owned." Then the real 3D kagiboy swings in, cartridge settling into the slot.

> So I picked up my old Game Boy again, and it hit me. It's the most honest screen I've ever owned. It doesn't go online. It doesn't run apps. It only does what my thumbs tell it to. What if it guarded my keys? That's kagiboy: a cartridge for the Game Boy you already have.

### 5 · How it feels
**Visual:** One 3D shot per step on the real console: mash, shake, 12 words, PIN, pair (same code on the phone), the request on screen, an extreme close-up of A held down, then Signed.

> Turning it on feels like a new save file. Mash the buttons, shake it, and that's your randomness. Write down twelve words. Pick a PIN. Your phone and the Game Boy show the same six digits, and A lets it in. After that, your phone can ask, but it can't decide. The cartridge reads every request itself and puts the amount and the address on that tiny screen. Nothing moves until you hold A.

### 6 · Try it
**Visual:** The live demo in a browser frame, then the swap screen on the console with the six chain logos.

> Don't take my word for it. Open kagiboy.xyz/demo. The same Game Boy software runs in your browser, next to the phone app. It signs testnet transactions on six chains, from Solana to Robinhood Chain, and swaps through SODAX, every swap checked on the Game Boy first.

### 7 · Inside
**Visual:** The cartridge lifts out of the console and comes apart; the four chips are named on cards.

> Inside, a secure chip that never lets your keys out, a chip that talks to the Game Boy, and Bluetooth that only carries what's public. Five wrong PINs and it forgets everything. Your twelve words bring it all back, in any normal wallet.

### 8 · Who it's for
**Visual:** 118.69M Game Boys sold (source: Wikipedia); a slow 3D turntable; the four products as cards (prices on screen, not spoken).

> I'm making this for people like me, kids who grew up with a Game Boy and somehow ended up in crypto. A hundred and eighteen million Game Boys were sold. This isn't something you hide away. It's something you put on a shelf, or give to someone you love. And I live in Japan, where these consoles still turn up at flea markets.

### 9 · Close
**Visual:** The roadmap line, the closing line, then the console with "press start" and the end card.

> Next, it leaves the browser: a real cartridge, then our own board, and two outside security audits before anyone can buy one. Crypto shouldn't feel like something you're scared of. It should feel like that day at the flea market. kagiboy. Press start.
